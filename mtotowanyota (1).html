What We Do

Creating a compassionate
space for healing

We provide support, resources, and a loving community for families navigating the profound loss of a baby born still — so every tiny life is seen, named, and remembered with dignity.

🕊️

Remember

A memorial space where families can remember their precious babies. Their lives were real, their love was real — and they will never be forgotten, however briefly they were here.

💙

Support

Connecting bereaved families with trained grief counsellors, peer support groups, and compassionate care — available around the clock, across Kenya and East Africa.

🌟

Celebrate

Every life matters, however brief. We celebrate the profound, lasting impact these tiny souls have on the families who carry them always in their hearts.

Who We Are

A promise of enduring
love for every family

Mtoto Wa Nyota was founded by bereaved parents, grief counsellors, and community leaders in Kenya. We believe that stillbirth must be spoken about openly — with dignity, without shame — and that no family should walk through grief alone.

Our work is community-funded and entirely free for all families. We operate 24/7 across Kenya and East Africa, partnering with 42 hospitals and hospices to reach families at their most vulnerable.

Every candle lit, every story shared, every counselling session — all made possible by the generosity of people who believe that every tiny life deserves to be remembered.

Your Path

The healing journey,
step by gentle step

Grief has no timeline. But you are never walking it alone. Here is how we walk beside you.

Step 01
Reach Out

Contact our 24/7 support line the moment you feel ready. There is no right time, no wrong words. Our compassionate team will meet you exactly where you are.

🤝
💬
Step 02
Connect with a Counsellor

Be matched with a trained perinatal bereavement counsellor who specialises in stillbirth. Completely free, confidential, and available in English and Swahili.

Step 03
Join a Support Circle

Meet other families who truly understand. Our warm, private circles meet monthly in-person across Kenya and online — a community built on shared understanding.

🌸
🕯️
Step 04
Remember & Cherish

Light a candle, share your story, add your baby to our memorial garden. Because remembering is not staying stuck — it is a profound act of love that continues forever.
